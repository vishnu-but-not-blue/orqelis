Where our information comes from.

Public procurement data

Procurement notices are sourced from Tenders Electronic Daily (TED), published by the Publications Office of the European Union. Source notices and versions are linked within each opportunity. We retain retrieval dates, checksums and parser versions to make our analysis traceable.

See the TED legal notice and official Search API documentation. Reuse is subject to source exceptions and third-party rights. We avoid unnecessary personal information and do not use TED logos.

Our analysis is independent

{{ config.app_name }} is not an EU institution and is not endorsed by TED or the European Union. Eligibility assessments and business estimates are independently generated advisory outputs. Official notices and procurement documents remain authoritative.

Facts, evidence and uncertainty

Source facts retain their original text and locators. Extracted requirements carry confidence and review states. Company profile claims remain self-declared until linked documentary evidence is confirmed. Estimates use displayed assumptions. Missing data is marked unknown. No score is a guarantee of eligibility or a probability of winning.

Freshness and examples

Every opportunity displays its retrieval date. During upstream interruptions, previously retrieved notices remain available. Demonstration records are explicitly labeled as example data and are not published procurement opportunities. Translations, if supplied, are for convenience; original-language text remains authoritative.
